02 — Senior thesis & defense

Biogenic Building Materials and Urban Heat Island Mitigation

Mawar Sharon Christian School Research Project · August 2025–June 2026

"Evaluating Biogenic Building Materials' Role in Singapore's Urban Heat Island Effect Reduction and Comparison to Surabaya's City Infrastructure" — authored and defended, with structured primary-research interviews with Alvin Chia (Urban Development, Sentosa Island, Singapore) and Prof. Dr. Ir. Surjamanto Wonorahardjo (Head, Building Technology Research Group, Institut Teknologi Bandung). Both experts, independently, recommended prioritizing biogenic materials in secondary structures (roofs, walls) over primary ones, citing Indonesia's differing structural lifespan regulations (100-year primary vs. 20–30-year secondary replacement cycles) as a lower-risk entry point for real-world adoption. Presented internally at the MSCS thesis defense and externally at a TED-Ed Student Talks Q&A session at Pakuwon Mall, Surabaya.
